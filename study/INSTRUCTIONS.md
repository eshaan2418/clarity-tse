# Instructions for participants (read aloud by the experimenter)

1. Put on the headphones. If anything ever sounds uncomfortably loud, take them off and tell me.
2. In every recording, one person is the **target**: the voice from the short "this is who to listen
   to" sample played first, coming from straight ahead.
3. **Words task:** listen once, then type every word the target said. Guess if you're unsure.
   Spelling doesn't matter much.
4. **Effort:** say how hard it was to understand (1 = no effort, 7 = extreme effort).
5. **Quality task:** you'll see several versions of the same recording. Rate each from 0 to 100
   for overall quality. One of them is the original clean voice; give that one a high score when
   you find it.
