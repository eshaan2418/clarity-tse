Listening study

Research prototype — not a medical device or hearing test. Take the headphones off if anything is too loud. You can stop at any time without giving a reason.

Participant code:

Experimenter: play the calibration noise and set the headphone level so the sound-level meter on the coupler reads 65 dB(A). Do not change the level afterwards.

Part 1 — trial /

You will hear one sentence spoken by the talker in front of you, once.

Type every word you heard that talker say:

How much effort did listening take? (1 = none … 7 = extreme)

Part 2 — screen /

Each version contains the same sentence. Rate the overall quality of each one against the Reference (0 = bad … 100 = excellent). One version is the hidden reference itself, so rate at least one at 100. You can replay as often as you like.
