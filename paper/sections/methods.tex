\section{Methods}
\subsection{Streaming extractor $f$ and latency budgets $\ell$}
Per frame the network receives log-power of both microphones, the cosine/sine of the interaural phase
difference, a directional feature $\cos(\mathrm{IPD}-\omega\tau(\theta))$ for the policy's azimuth
(zeros plus a flag when unknown), and is FiLM-conditioned on the enrollment embedding (zeros plus a flag
when no voice is enrolled). A causal TCN (2 repeats $\times$ 7 dilated blocks, bottleneck 128, hidden 256;
about 2\,M parameters) predicts complex per-microphone weights bounded to $|W|\le 1$ whose sum over
microphones is the output (a learned, time-varying MISO beamformer + mask): the model can attenuate but
not amplify a time-frequency bin, which is our ``fail by attenuating'' prior. Streaming inference keeps
explicit per-layer state; chunked inference reproduces full-sequence causal output (unit tests,
max deviation below $2\cdot 10^{-4}$; ONNX vs PyTorch below $10^{-6}$).
Budgets: hop $H\in\{16,40,80,128,160,320\}$ samples, i.e.\ 2, 5, 10, 16, 20, 40\,ms algorithmic latency,
plus a non-causal ceiling (centred convolutions, wider network) that also serves as the distillation teacher.
\paragraph{Training.} 40k pre-rendered 4\,s binaural mixtures from 4000 training geometries
(train-clean-100 speakers, ESC-50 folds 1--3, synthetic noise), SNR $-5$ to $15$\,dB. Conditioning
augmentations: enrolled-but-absent target (reference $=$ silence, 8\%), no voice (15\%), no direction
(15\%), DOA noise $\sigma=5^\circ$. Loss: soft-thresholded SNR (absent examples: output-energy loss)
\citep{wisdom2020mixit} $+$ multi-resolution compressed-STFT L1 with an asymmetric over-estimation penalty
(``artifact loss'') $+$ 0.5 $\times$ distillation SNR to the teacher. 6000 steps (teacher 8000), batch 16,
AdamW, cosine schedule; 2 and 5\,ms models are initialised from the 10\,ms model (``latency-aware
curriculum''). Model selection on the dev-clean validation set only.
\subsection{Attention-steered selection $\tau$}
Tracks of active talkers are maintained in the world frame (IMU yaw $+$ SRP-PHAT lateral DOA over a
0.4\,s window, mapped with the mean yaw over the same window). The attended track maximises
$p(\text{face}\mid\theta_j)\cdot v_j\cdot a_j$: a Gaussian facing prior on the head-relative azimuth,
a bounded voice-similarity factor $v_j$ (ECAPA cosine of a delay-and-sum beam towards the track vs the
enrolled voices), and an activity term. Switching requires a posterior ratio above 1.5 held for 0.3\,s;
a silent current target is held for 3\,s. Fallbacks: no enrollment $\Rightarrow$ direction only;
single microphone $\Rightarrow$ voice only; no IMU $\Rightarrow$ head-frame tracks. The three free
parameters were tuned on validation-split scenarios only.
\subsection{Listener personalisation $\rho$}
For an audiogram at 250\,Hz--8\,kHz, a small MLP outputs per-band gain (initialised at the half-gain
rule), compression ratio and knee for 24 ERB bands, applied as real gains in the extractor's STFT, with
per-band gain capped at 35\,dB before the global limiter. It is trained on extractor outputs for
training-split mixtures over random audiograms with a differentiable envelope-correlation proxy under a
simple audibility model (\emph{not} HASPI, to avoid training on the evaluation metric); the
per-listener variant fine-tunes per-band offsets for one audiogram. Baselines: NAL-R (pyclarity,
220-tap linear-phase FIR, 6.9\,ms delay, compensated for scoring) on the mixture, and NAL-R after the
extractor.
\subsection{Evaluation}
Frozen test set: 240 scenes (restaurant, party, street, kitchen $\times$ SNR $\{-5,0,5,10,15\}$\,dB
$\times$ 12), test-clean speakers and ESC-50 fold 5, 6\,s, slow head drift, 10\,s enrollment; per-array
SHA-256 checksums. Metrics against the target's direct+50\,ms early image at the left ear: SI-SDRi,
PESQ-WB, STOI/ESTOI, HASPI v2 (normal hearing, or the profile's audiogram for $\rho$), DNSMOS. 95\%
bootstrap CIs over scenes; paired Wilcoxon tests with Holm correction.
